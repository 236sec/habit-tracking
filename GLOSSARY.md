# Habit Tracking

Language for an individual's habits and daily focus.

## Language

**Habit**:
A recurring behavior an individual plans for every day or selected weekdays. Its progress is judged against those planned days.
_Avoid_: Task

**Planned day**:
A calendar day on which a habit is scheduled in the individual's local time zone. A schedule change affects future planned days and does not rewrite earlier ones.

**Habit check-in**:
A record that a habit was completed on a planned day. An individual may correct a past check-in.
_Avoid_: Task completion

**Active habit**:
A habit available for new check-ins. The free plan permits up to three active habits.

**Archived habit**:
A habit removed from the active list while its earlier planned days and check-ins remain in history.

**Free plan**:
The ongoing access level with up to three active habits, one morning routine, all task features, reminders, and four weeks of statistics.

**Subscription**:
Monthly paid access that removes the active-habit limit and makes the full statistics history available. Access lasts through the paid period after cancellation.

**Completion rate**:
The share of planned habit days that have a completed check-in over a given period. The current progress cue uses the last 28 calendar days.

**Target completion rate**:
A percentage chosen for each habit as a reference for judging its completion rate. The interface distinguishes progress at or above the target from progress below it.
_Avoid_: Score

**Task**:
A one-time action with a due day. Tasks, rather than habits, are organized in the Eisenhower matrix using importance and urgency chosen by the individual.
_Avoid_: Habit

**Overdue task**:
An unfinished task whose due day has passed. It remains available until completed or removed.

**Morning routine**:
An ordered sequence of steps an individual follows after waking. It starts fresh each local day without carrying unfinished steps forward, and today's routine is finished when each step is completed or skipped.
_Avoid_: Task list

**Routine step**:
One action in a morning routine, marked complete or skipped as the individual moves through the sequence. A step may stand alone or link to a habit; completing a linked step checks in that habit when the day is planned for it, while skipping does not.
